Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026.

HelgoIQ provides software for fitness and wellness studio operators. This policy explains how we collect and use information submitted through this website, related enquiry workflows, and Meta (Facebook and Instagram) business integrations connected to HelgoIQ.

Who we are

HELGOIQ LIMITED (Company No. 17245104) is a private limited company registered in England and Wales. Registered office: Rooks Nest Farm, Weston Road, Stevenage, Hertfordshire, SG1 4DE. Contact: hello@helgoiq.com. Privacy: privacy@helgoiq.com.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, studio or company name, email address, phone number, enquiry details, and communication preferences when you contact us.

How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, arrange walkthroughs, provide product information, operate the website, improve our services, and comply with legal obligations.

SMS and phone information

If you provide a mobile number and choose to receive SMS messages, we may use that number to contact you about your enquiry, appointment, account, or service-related updates. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out by replying STOP.

Message frequency varies based on your enquiry, appointment, or account activity.

Mobile opt-in data and consent are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes.

See also our SMS Communication Policy.

Meta, Facebook and Instagram

When a studio owner connects Facebook, Instagram or other Meta business assets to HelgoIQ, we may receive and process information needed to provide messaging, inbox, and related integration features. Depending on the permissions granted and features enabled, this can include:

  • Page, Instagram professional account, and business asset identifiers
  • Message content and metadata exchanged with the connected inbox tools
  • Sender identifiers, timestamps, delivery status, and conversation context
  • Connection tokens and configuration required to keep the integration working

HelgoIQ processes this information to operate the integration for the connecting studio, provide support, maintain security, and meet legal obligations. Studios remain responsible for how they communicate with their customers through connected channels. Meta products remain subject to Meta’s terms and policies.

To disconnect Meta or request deletion of Meta-connected data, follow the instructions at Meta data deletion, or email compliance@helgoiq.com.

Sharing

We may use trusted service providers for hosting, email, SMS (including Twilio where configured), payments, analytics, customer support, and communications. They may process information only as needed to provide services to HelgoIQ. We do not sell personal data.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete enquiry information by contacting privacy@helgoiq.com or hello@helgoiq.com.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@helgoiq.com.
