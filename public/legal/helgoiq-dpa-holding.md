## 1. Parties and roles

1.1 The **Customer** (studio / gym / fitness business) is the **Data Controller**.  
1.2 **HELGOIQ LIMITED** (“HelgoIQ”, “Processor”) is the **Data Processor** in respect of Customer Personal Data processed in the HelgoIQ platform on the Customer’s documented instructions.  
1.3 HelgoIQ acts as an independent controller for its own marketing-site, account, billing-contact, and support records as described in the Privacy Policy.

## 2. Subject matter and duration

2.1 HelgoIQ processes Customer Personal Data to provide the HelgoIQ software and related support.  
2.2 Processing continues for the term of the Customer’s subscription and any post-termination retention/export window described in the Privacy Policy or Order.

## 3. Nature and purpose of processing

Typical processing includes hosting, storage, retrieval, transmission, and deletion of data used for:

- Member / lead profiles and communications  
- Bookings, attendance, memberships, and packs  
- Payment metadata via payment processors  
- Staff accounts and operational workflows  
- AI-assisted suggestions subject to Customer configuration and human approval controls  

## 4. Types of personal data and data subjects

**Data subjects:** members, leads, staff, teachers, and other individuals whose data the Customer uploads or collects via HelgoIQ.  

**Data types:** identity and contact data; booking/attendance; membership and purchase history; communications content; limited payment metadata; security/log data.  

**Special category data:** not required by HelgoIQ by default. If the Customer stores such data, the Customer is responsible for a lawful basis and additional safeguards.

## 5. Processor obligations

HelgoIQ shall:

- process Customer Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Customer (including these Terms/DPA and product configuration), unless required by law  
- ensure persons authorised to process are bound by confidentiality  
- implement appropriate technical and organisational measures  
- only engage sub-processors under written terms imposing equivalent protections, and maintain a public sub-processor list  
- assist the Customer, taking into account the nature of processing, with data subject requests and DPIAs where reasonable  
- delete or return Customer Personal Data after end of services, subject to legal retention and backup cycles  
- make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance and allow audits under agreed procedures  

## 6. Customer obligations

The Customer shall:

- ensure it has a lawful basis for processing and for instructing HelgoIQ  
- configure consents (including SMS/email marketing) appropriately  
- not instruct HelgoIQ to process data unlawfully  
- remain responsible for its staff access rights inside the product  

## 7. Sub-processors

7.1 The Customer authorises HelgoIQ to use the sub-processors listed at https://helgoiq.com/subprocessors (as updated from time to time).  
7.2 HelgoIQ will provide notice of material sub-processor changes via the Trust centre / email to account owners where practicable.  
7.3 The Customer may object on reasonable data-protection grounds; the parties will discuss in good faith.

## 8. International transfers

Where Customer Personal Data is transferred outside the UK/EEA, HelgoIQ will ensure appropriate transfer mechanisms (e.g. UK IDTA / EU SCCs) with relevant providers.

## 9. Security

HelgoIQ maintains measures appropriate to the risk, including encryption in transit, access controls, authentication via identity providers, and monitoring. A security summary is available via the Trust centre / on request.

## 10. Breach notification

HelgoIQ will notify the Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data, with information reasonably available to assist the Customer’s own notification duties.

## 11. Liability and precedence

Liability remains as set out in the HelgoIQ Terms of Service, except where non-excludable under applicable data protection law. If there is a conflict between this DPA and the Terms regarding data protection, this DPA prevails for that subject matter.

## 12. Governing law

This DPA is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
