## The honest read

HelgoIQ is the weakest of the three at *showing*. Feature detail pages (`/features/crm`, `/booking`, `/memberships`, `/ai`) are a green band, a paragraph, and bullets. Home has screenshots, but they are unannotated admin frames beside essays. That is why the site feels flat even when images exist.

Where we are already stronger:

- Public prices and a free Core. Walla starts at $320/location. LegitFit hides price behind a demo.
- A self-serve trial. Neither competitor puts “start now” on the homepage.
- Website import with a 30-day ranking window — neither of them leads with that.
- Unstaffed 24/7 and native door access. Boutique-only Walla does not own this.
- AI that cannot send without approval. WallaPredict is a churn flag; we should show the approval lock in the picture.
- We do not invent 110% growth or 800+ studios. Keep that. Fill slots when numbers are real.
