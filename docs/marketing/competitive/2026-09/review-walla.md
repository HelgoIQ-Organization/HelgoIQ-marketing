# Walla — review (desktop 1440 + phone 390)

Captured 6 September 2026. Screenshots: [assets/walla/](./assets/walla/). Inspiration only.

Primary crawl: home (full scroll), `/solutions`, `/features/studio-management`, `/features/predict`, `/solutions/retain`, `/pricing`, `/made-for/pilates` (and sibling Made For IA), demo/switch CTAs. Customer stories are homepage cards plus “Read all stories”. Resources = blog teaser + support centre.

## 1. Feature-explanation patterns

| Device | What it explains | Why it works | HelgoIQ fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lifestyle photo + short benefit, not a dashboard | “This is a studio, not a gym-chain SKU” | Emotion first; product is implied | Vertical cards — we already do photos (pending). Need a **product scene** in the card, not only the floor |
| Four pillar cards (Manage / Grow / Retain / Insights) | The whole platform in one scan | Named jobs, not a 26-feature dump | We have six icon pillars of similar density — too many words inside each |
| Numbered **Meet → Migrate → Train → Grow** with photos | How you start | Reduces “this will take months” fear | `/migrate` and Home: we have numbered text, no pictures |
| Rotating / in-situ **app mock** in the hero (classes, intro, messages) | Member-facing product | Shows the branded app without a feature tour | We do not sell a consumer app as the hero; our equivalent is the public booking site |
| WallaPredict as a named object | Churn before they cancel | AI has a face (a product name + a next step) | Command Centre ask/answer — show the question and the list, plus the approval lock |
| Video chapters on `/solutions` (0:00 platform, 2:15 scheduling, 5:30 marketing, 8:45 Predict) | Depth without a wall of text | Time-boxed | Our “See it working” modal is still a placeholder |
| “Already on Mindbody, Momence, Arketa…” band | Switch | Names the incumbent | We mention TeamUp in migrate FAQ. Should name the stacks independents actually use, without fake volume claims |

They are **weaker than LegitFit** at flow diagrams and hanging artefacts. They are **stronger** at photography, serif confidence, and “we are you” copy.

## 2. Visual language

- **Colour:** Bright Walla blue, black pill “Get Started”, lots of white. Occasional light-blue bands.
- **Type:** Serif headlines (premium, “boutique”) + clean sans body. This is the closest cousin to our Cormorant + Manrope pairing — they use the serif for *every* H1; we mostly save Cormorant for the wordmark and quotes.
- **Spacing:** Airy. Cards with light shadow. 8–10 viewport homepage.
- **Photography:** Warm, real studios, people. Product UI is secondary.
- **Iconography:** Simple line icons on pillars.
- **Motion:** Hero lifestyle rotation; app content rotation.
- **White space:** More than LegitFit, much more than our text walls.

## 3. Page architecture

Home: **hero + dual CTA in the first screen** → logo cloud → “How Walla Approaches It” (four values) → four pillars → Walla Way (4 steps) + migration callout → Made For (8 modalities) → testimonials → **pricing teaser with dollar amounts on the homepage** → blog → dark closing CTA → newsletter → footer.

First CTA: immediate (`Get a Quote` / `Start Today` / `Book a Demo` depending on the build; secondary **Make the Switch**).

Features split: Platform pillars → `/solutions/*` and `/features/*` (Predict, studio-management, retain). Not one URL per atomic feature like LegitFit.

Segment by **modality** (yoga, pilates, barre, lagree, cycling, circuit, franchise, multi-location). Explicitly **not** spas, salons, or gym chains. That is a positioning choice. HelgoIQ must not pretend to be boutique-only — we also sell 24/7 unstaffed.

## 4. Proof and trust

- Logo cloud of real boutique names (Motivate, LVL, Jane Do, Yoga Pod, etc.).
- Named quotes with headshots (Shannon, Naomi, Justin, Meghan, Whitney, Allison).
- G2 and Capterra 4.9 badges in the hero.
- Solutions page claims: 40% admin time, 3× faster training, 4.8★ G2, 800+ studios.
- “After 13 years on Mindbody, we knew how to use Walla after 13 minutes.”
- Pricing page: onboarding packages, migration timeline 4–8 weeks.

Again: their numbers are theirs. We do not match them with fiction.

## 5. Calls to action

- **Book a Free Demo / Book a Demo** and **Make the Switch**. Header **Get Started**.
- Pricing is public ($320 Core, $599 Pro, Enterprise custom). Pro is “Talk to a studio specialist”.
- **No self-serve trial** on the homepage.
- Demo is sold as “See Walla in 5 minutes. No hard sell.”
- Add-ons (branded app $149, website/SEO, premium onboarding) are visible — they are not afraid of a price list.

## 6. Copy voice

Empathetic, insider, benefit-first. “The studio you built deserves software that keeps up.” “Built for boutique fitness. Period.” Sentences are medium length, warmer than LegitFit, less operational than HelgoIQ. They name competitors. Feature names are jobs: Retain, Grow, WallaPredict.

## 7. Mobile

- Hero photo stacks under the serif headline; CTAs remain two buttons.
- Pillars become stacked cards; still scannable.
- Made For becomes a vertical list of large photos — works, but long.
- Pricing cards stack; $320 / $599 stay readable.
- They degrade by **stacking pictures**, not by turning pictures into extra paragraphs. That is the lesson.

## 8. Gaps they cover that we do not

- **Switch from Mindbody** as a first-class nav item and homepage band.
- Customer stories index with named studios.
- Resources / blog as a growth engine (we have a blog; it is quieter).
- Branded consumer app as a pictured product.
- Franchise / multi-location as its own Made For page (we have `/for/multi-site` — thinner).
- Public dollar pricing on the **homepage**, not only `/pricing`.

We already cover: cheaper public GBP pricing, free trial, website SEO migration, 24/7, human-approval AI, founding-studio honesty.
