### Where ours is already better

1. **Price on the page, and a free start.** Independents can decide without a 30-minute demo.
2. **Honesty architecture.** Null metrics hide. Competitors would rather invent.
3. **Website as the product.** Paste the site, protect rankings 30 days. Neither competitor makes that the hero.
4. **24/7 unstaffed + native door.** Walla’s boutique-only line would reject Active 247.
5. **Governance.** “AI drafted this; you send it” is a better story than WallaPredict’s black box — if we *show* the lock.
6. **UK independent price point.** £149 / £249 vs $320 / $599 before add-ons.

### Where we are weakest (be direct)

- We *tell*. They *show*. That is the whole gap Declan named.
- Feature detail pages are the poorest pages on the site.
- Proof is a correctly empty box. A correctly empty box still looks like a new, unproven vendor.
- Hero is atmosphere. The split visual is still a labelled placeholder.
- We go quiet in the middle of Home — no CTA between “in the product” and founding studios.
- We do not name the incumbent on Home the way Walla names Mindbody.

## 10. What both share (safer bets — do these first)

1. A working picture beside the promise (product chrome or app-in-hand — not only landscape).
2. CTA in the first viewport.
3. Studio-type segmentation with a visual, not a paragraph.
4. Named customers on the homepage (we have names; we lack photos and numbers).
5. A four-step “how you start” that is pictured.
6. The same CTA repeated after major blocks (without changing the *kind* of door).
7. Feature pages that still *show* the artefact (message, roster, pay sheet), not only a bullet list.

## 11. Where they differ — what suits HelgoIQ

| Difference | LegitFit | Walla | HelgoIQ should |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who they sell | Wide: gyms, hotels, leisure, SGPT | Boutique only | Independents **including** pilates, boutique, PT, wellness, **and** 24/7. Do not copy Walla’s “period.” |
| Price | Hidden | High and public | Keep public GBP + free Core. It is a weapon. |
| Door | Demo | Demo + switch | **Trial + demo.** Hero may lead Bring my site for migration-intent. Features/Pricing keep trial + demo. |
| Proof style | Big percentages | Named studios + ratings | Founder quote now; metrics when real; never 110%. |
| AI | Light / not the hero | WallaPredict named | Command Centre as a pictured Q&A with approval. |

**Lead with a trial, not a demo-gated quote.** Small independent studios will not book a sales call as the only way in. Walla and LegitFit can afford that funnel because they sell onboarding packages and higher ARPU. We should not.

Demo stays for people who want a walkthrough (Enterprise, multi-site, anxious migrators). Pricing stays on `/pricing` and can be teased on Home as it already is.
